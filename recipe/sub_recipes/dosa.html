Dosa Recipe

This is How you make Dosa kids

Ingredients Needed

  • The Batter: 2 cups readymade Dosa batter (bring it to room temperature)
  • Cooking: 2–3 tbsp oil or ghee (for crisping)
  • Optional Finish: A dollop of butter or a sprinkle of podi (gunpowder spice)

Instructions

  1. Heat the Pan

    2. Place a non-stick or well-seasoned cast-iron tawa (griddle) over medium heat. To check the temperature, sprinkle a few drops of water on it—if they sizzle and evaporate instantly, the pan is ready. Wipe the pan clean with a damp cloth or a piece of paper towel.

  2. Pour and Spread

    3. Turn the heat down to low. Pour a large ladleful of batter right into the center of the pan. Using the back of the ladle, quickly but gently spiral the batter outward in a circular motion to form a thin, even pancake.

  3. Crisp and Roll

    4. Turn the heat up to medium-high. Drizzle half a teaspoon of oil or ghee around the edges and over the center of the dosa. Cook for 2 minutes until the edges start lifting and the bottom turns a beautiful golden brown. Flip if you like a softer texture, or simply fold it in half straight out of the pan for maximum crispiness.

EAT WHEN HOT AND CRISPY...YOU WILL LOVE IT!
