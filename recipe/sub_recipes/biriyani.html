BIRIYANI

Biriyani Recipe

This is How you make Biriyani kids

Ingredients Needed

  • Chicken & Prep: 750g chicken pieces, 2 cups Basmati rice (soaked for 20 mins and drained)
  • Aromatics: 2 sliced onions, 2 chopped tomatoes, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, a handful of mint and coriander leaves
  • Liquids:3 tbsp oil, 2 tbsp ghee, 3 tbsp yogurt (curd), 2¾ cups water, juice of ½ lemon
  • Spices:1 bay leaf, 1 cinnamon stick, 3 cardamoms, 4 cloves, 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp turmeric, 2 tbsp store-bought Biryani Masala, and salt

Instructions

  1. Quick Marination

    2. In a bowl, mix the chicken with the yogurt, chili powder, turmeric, biryani masala, and a pinch of salt. Set it aside while you prep the pot

  2. Sauté Aromatics

    3. Heat the oil and ghee in your pressure cooker or deep pot over medium heat. Add the whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamoms, cloves) and onions. Cook until the onions turn golden brown, then stir in the ginger-garlic paste for 1 minute. Add the tomatoes, mint, and coriander leaves, cooking until the tomatoes soften.

  3. Sear the Chicken

    4. Add the marinated chicken to the pot. Sauté on medium-high heat for about 5 minutes until the chicken changes color, then lower the heat, cover, and let it cook in its own juices for 8 minutes.

  4. Add Rice & Cook

    5. Pour in the 2¾ cups of water and bring it to a boil. Taste the water and add salt (it should taste slightly salty). Gently stir in the soaked rice and lemon juice.
